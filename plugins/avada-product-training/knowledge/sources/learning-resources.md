# Curated learning resources

Use this index before scanning all Chase source cards. Every source includes an active viewing prompt and learner artifact.

## 1. Metric diagnosis — one combo, one job

- **Source:** Chase Chappell — If You Watch One Facebook Ad Video
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/42uhZYnyEXU
- **Start:** `t=9:36` for flat sales + high ROAS; full video for the diagnosis sequence.
- **Notice:** which metric combo assigns the problem to ads, site, AOV, returning customer or subscription.
- **Prompt:** For each scenario, answer: “What is the quickest discriminating job—and what generic answer is the trap?”
- **Artifact:** one-page metric → diagnosis → job tree applied to one store.

## 2. Brand stage and operating system

- **Source:** Chase Chappell — 5 Levels of Brands
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/6IAgiYdUlXM
- **Notice:** Survival → Systems → Identity → Distribution → Icon; operating behavior, not revenue label.
- **Prompt:** Which level is the chosen brand in? Name two observed behaviors and one behavior it should not copy from another level.
- **Artifact:** stage diagnosis with evidence and next constraint.

## 3. Offer economics

- **Source:** Chase Chappell — Ecommerce Offer Economics
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/RHZ39WfEuM0
- **Notice:** margin is traded only for conversion, larger carts or repeat; cash timing matters.
- **Prompt:** Which offer changes CVR, AOV or repeat? What contribution does it create after discount/COGS/fulfillment?
- **Artifact:** downside/base/upside contribution model for one live offer.

## 4. Product/market story and hero mechanism

- **Source:** Chase Chappell — 100M Offer on Shopify
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/mJ57WQM_eCo
- **Notice:** named reason the product works when alternatives have not; not merely a larger discount.
- **Prompt:** What failed alternative/enemy does the mechanism replace? Is the claim credible and provable?
- **Artifact:** mechanism statement + evidence/unknown table.

## 5. Business model — low-cost airline

- **Source:** Các hãng hàng không giá rẻ kiếm tiền bằng cách nào?
- **Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZDPlZBK6Oac
- **Channel:** CD Media - Why?
- **Notice:** value proposition, activity/cost system and ancillary revenue reinforce each other.
- **Prompt:** Which activities are reduced, eliminated, raised and created? Where is value captured?
- **Artifact:** Business Model in One Page for the airline model.

## 6. Strategy as choices and trade-offs

- **Source:** Steve Jobs talking about a Macbook in 1983!
- **Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vk3vo7xVkJc
- **Channel:** Foundr
- **Duration:** 0:47
- **Notice:** a focused product/market choice is visible even in a short explanation.
- **Prompt:** What is the explicit customer, job and trade-off? What is excluded?
- **Artifact:** one-sentence Where-to-Play / How-to-Win hypothesis.

## 7. Category/functional POD — Volvo

- **Source:** Volvo EX60 — New standards in safety
- **Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4H8YUng7QPo
- **Channel:** Volvo Cars
- **Notice:** functional difference becomes a repeated category memory structure.

## 8. Experience business model — Airbnb

- **Source:** 1/2 Billion Guest Arrival — Belong Anywhere
- **Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rQo6zr1nHvw

## 9. Value innovation — Cirque du Soleil

## 10. Distinctive brand case — Yellow Tail

- **Source:** BIG GAME Commercial
- **Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nMA8CyRimZo
- **Channel:** [yellow tail]
- **Notice:** accessible category entry, distinctive assets and mass-market choice architecture.
- **Prompt:** What complexity is removed? What cue makes the brand easy to notice and remember?

## Usage rule

Do not assign a passive playlist. Choose one source matched to the learner's mission and require its artifact before the next lesson.
