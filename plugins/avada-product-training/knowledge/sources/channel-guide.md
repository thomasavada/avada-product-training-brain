# Channel guide — what to follow and why

This is a curated starting set, not a feed to consume passively. Pick a channel based on the capability you are trying to build.

## Chase Chappell

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCIGEBB32IQdzLOhEtlSP06g
- Best for: metric diagnosis, offers, Meta ads, creative, TikTok Shop, CRO and retention.
- Use when: you need to connect one metric combination to one operational job.
- Watch rule: claims and numbers remain creator claims until verified.
- Artifact after watching: a metric → diagnosis → job map applied to one live store.

## Operators Podcast

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCuGneytUApsb7SEynqoZ0ug
- Best for: P&L, cash, inventory, SKU complexity, hiring, wholesale, founder wealth and operating leverage.
- Use when: website analysis is too shallow and you need to think like an operator/owner.
- Watch rule: separate the guest's company context from a universal rule.
- Artifact after watching: one operating decision, its economics and the condition under which it would fail.

## Limited Supply

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCKmdas65668BmI97lBlh4uw
- Best for: DTC brand building, operations, creative, team, scaling and founder decisions.
- Use when: comparing how different operators solve growth constraints.
- Watch rule: founder anecdotes are case evidence, not benchmark truth.
- Artifact after watching: compare the episode's playbook with one brand at a different stage.

## Shopify Masters

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCLicUpeWYLe0zSCiIdZKv_g
- Best for: founder stories, product/category choices, distribution and Shopify merchant journeys.
- Use when: collecting diverse business-model cases.
- Watch rule: published success stories carry survivorship and editorial selection bias.
- Artifact after watching: fact/inference/unknown table plus one stage diagnosis.

## The Unofficial Shopify Podcast / Ethercycle

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCbonrAHrxSXtVcdJRgOHcBA
- Best for: Shopify platform changes, merchant operations, agencies, conversion and implementation realities.
- Use when: connecting strategy to what merchants can actually run on Shopify.
- Watch rule: check current Shopify documentation before treating platform details as current.
- Artifact after watching: one platform change → merchant impact → product opportunity map.

## The CPG Guys

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCOwPKstTze6HCzWuAG3Nleg
- Best for: CPG, retail, omnichannel, CRM, distribution and enterprise commerce.
- Use when: a DTC-only lens misses retail and channel economics.
- Watch rule: enterprise playbooks may be premature for Survival/Growth merchants.
- Artifact after watching: channel role map and one DTC-vs-retail trade-off.

## Chew On This

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCPBpCJWqLdbNPDKJVBAigYg
- Best for: DTC founder/operator interviews and practical growth stories.
- Use when: you need a contemporary operator case to challenge a framework.
- Watch rule: verify promotional claims and distinguish the guest's retrospective story from causal proof.
- Artifact after watching: one claim to verify, one useful mechanism and one context limitation.

## Selection rule

Before opening YouTube, write the learning job:

```text
I need to improve ______
I will watch ______ because ______
I will produce ______
I will test it on ______
```

If there is no artifact, do not add the video to the queue.
