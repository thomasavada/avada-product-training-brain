## Evidence rules

- Prefer current primary source over transcript summary.
- Preserve date/version/commit when citing an artifact.
- A learner slide proves what the learner presented, not that every claim is true.
- A vendor case proves the vendor published the claim, not causal incrementality.
- Full raw transcripts contain ASR noise; quote only after checking the exact block/timestamp.
