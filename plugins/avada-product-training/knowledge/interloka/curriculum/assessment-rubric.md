# Assessment Rubric — Draft

> This is a synthesis scaffold. Final weights must be reconciled with the original course exercises and template.

| Criterion | Draft weight |
|---|---:|
| Evidence and research quality | 15 |
| Segment/customer/job clarity | 10 |
| Business-model coherence and economics | 15 |
| Competitive strategy and trade-offs | 10 |
| Positioning and Big Idea | 10 |
| Functional differentiation | 10 |
| Emotional differentiation | 8 |
| Culture/core-values alignment | 8 |
| Strategic coherence across the page | 9 |
| Executive defense and falsifiability | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Automatic red flags

- claims without provenance;
- future capability presented as current fact;
- business model reduced to pricing only;
- positioning written without segment/alternative/proof;
- values without observable behavior;
- one-page sections contradict each other;
- strategy has no trade-offs or explicit NOs.
