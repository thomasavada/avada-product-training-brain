# Language Style — Vietnamese-first, English technical terms

## Audience

Vietnamese PMs, CEOs/team leads, and product/business operators.

## Voice

- Vietnamese is the primary teaching language.
- Natural, close, direct, and practical—not academic translation.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete questions.
- Speak as a facilitator: “hãy thử”, “đặt câu hỏi”, “điểm cần kiểm tra”.
- Avoid corporate filler and machine-translated Vietnamese.

## Keep in English

Keep established technical terms when translating them would make the lesson less precise or less useful for real work:

The canonical first-use explanations live in `curriculum/translation-glossary.md`.

- Marketing
- Strategy / Integrated Brand Strategy
- Business Model / Business Model in One Page
- Big Why / Strategy Goals
- JTBD
- C-D Map / Centrality / Distinctiveness
- Business Model Spectrum / Selective / Sandwich / Big Mass
- Value Created / Value Delivered / Value Captured
- Operation Formula / Profit Formula
- Product–Market Fit
- Strategy Canvas
- Blue Ocean / Red Ocean / Value Innovation / ERRC
- Category Positioning / CEP
- POD / Reasons to Believe / RTB
- Brand Distinctiveness
- Brand Archetype / Brand Personality
- Core Values / Corporate Culture
- Positioning Big Idea

## First-use pattern

On first introduction, pair Vietnamese meaning with the technical term:

```text
Giá trị tạo ra (Value Created)
Công thức vận hành (Operation Formula)
Điểm khác biệt lý tính (Functional Differentiators)
Hình mẫu thương hiệu (Brand Archetype)
Ý tưởng lớn định vị (Positioning Big Idea)
```

After the term is established, use the English technical term consistently.

## Do not translate

- Brand names.
- Famous slogans.
- Video titles.
- URLs and QR destinations.
- Source image IDs.
- Framework names where translation changes meaning.

## Examples

Too stiff:

> “Sự đổi mới giá trị phá vỡ sự đánh đổi giá trị–chi phí.”

Preferred:

> “Value Innovation nghĩa là tạo thêm nhiều giá trị cho khách hàng hơn mức chi phí phải tăng.”

Too English-heavy:

> “Build a defensible POD through core competence and customer relevance.”

Preferred:

> “Muốn POD đứng vững, khác biệt đó phải liên quan tới khách hàng và được chống lưng bằng core competence thật.”
