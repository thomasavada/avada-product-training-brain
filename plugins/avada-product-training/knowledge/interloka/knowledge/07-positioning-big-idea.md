# Section 6 — Positioning Big Idea

**Source range:** `IMG_7203`–`IMG_7210`.

## [SOURCE] Brand stands for one thing

`IMG_7203` uses the Law of Focus: a powerful marketing concept is owning a word/idea in the prospect’s mind.

One approach is to turn a unique product difference/USP into the Big Idea. The source references classic associations such as cavities, overnight delivery, driving pleasure, and safety.

## [SOURCE] Sources of a Big Idea

The cases show multiple routes:

- unique product/service difference;
- business-model innovation;
- customer insight;
- category repositioning;
- brand archetype/cultural belief;
- a focused emotional or experiential promise.

## Global teaching cases

- **Volvo — Safety:** credible because product engineering/testing and communication repeatedly reinforce it.
- **BMW — Driving Pleasure:** the Big Idea matches a driver-oriented product experience.
- **FedEx — Overnight reliability:** a focused customer job backed by an expensive logistics system.
- **Mastercard — Priceless:** emotional territory elevates a functionally substitutable payment product.
- **Airbnb — Belong Anywhere:** connects marketplace model, host/guest identity, and emotional travel meaning.

See `case-studies/global/positioning-and-big-idea-global.md`.

## [SOURCE] Big Idea is not merely a slogan

The source sequence demonstrates:

```text
Business model + customer insight
→ positioning Big Idea
→ creative direction
→ slogan/execution
```

## [SYNTHESIS] Quality test

- Is the idea rooted in a business/customer truth?
- Can the company operationally deliver it?
- Is it focused enough to own?
- Does it guide product, service, culture, and communication?
- Is the slogan an expression of the idea rather than a substitute for it?
- Would the idea survive beyond one campaign?
