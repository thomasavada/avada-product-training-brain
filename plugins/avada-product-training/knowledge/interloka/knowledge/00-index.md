## Integrated system

The course combines:

1. **Business model** — create, deliver, and capture value.
2. **Competitive strategy** — choose a different value/cost/activity system.
3. **Category and brand strategy** — define what the business is, why it matters, and how it is remembered.
4. **Corporate culture** — connect leadership assumptions and core values to execution.
