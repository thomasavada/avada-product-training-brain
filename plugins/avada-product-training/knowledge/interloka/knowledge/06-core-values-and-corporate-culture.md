# Section 5 — Core Values & Corporate Culture

**Source range:** `IMG_7194`–`IMG_7202`.

## [SOURCE] Leadership and culture

The section opens with Jim Collins’ idea of getting the right people in the right seats and the principle that the process of discovering true core values matters more than selecting attractive words.

## [SOURCE] Edgar Schein’s three levels

`IMG_7196` references organizational culture and leadership:

1. **Artifacts** — what people see/hear; visible “culture theatre”.
2. **Espoused values** — stated values.
3. **Shared tacit assumptions** — the actual underlying essence of culture.

The section connects Jung’s Persona/Shadow and behavioral psychology to Schein’s basic assumptions: deep, often unconscious beliefs generate behavior and culture.

## [SOURCE] Leadership beliefs

`IMG_7197`–`IMG_7198` use leaders/literature to show how personal beliefs shape choices, including beliefs about competition, justice, discipline, and conquest. The goal is to uncover the leader’s real assumptions rather than copy generic corporate values.

## [SOURCE] Natural vs expected values

`IMG_7200` provides a template:

- list natural/unconscious values already present;
- list expected/aspirational values;
- identify up to three naturally shared values that fit the organization and are easy to enact.

## [SOURCE] Expression of values

The course prefers expressive compound nouns/verb phrases over generic labels, with examples such as:

- respect for the individual;
- provide the best customer service;
- pursuit of excellence;
- adapt quickly;
- focus on people;
- spirit of responsibility;
- can-do spirit;
- safety.

`IMG_7201` warns that many companies list similar words; expression and behavior must make the organization identifiable.

## [SYNTHESIS] Operationalization

Each value should define:

- underlying belief/basic assumption;
- observable behaviors;
- anti-behaviors/shadow;
- leadership decision it resolves;
- rituals and incentives;
- trade-off the organization is willing to make.

Culture is not a poster. It is the repeated behavior generated by shared assumptions.
