# Business Model in One Page

> Status: capstone shell. Populate only after source mapping is complete.

## Principle

The one-page is not a form to fill from imagination. It is a compression of research and strategic choices.

## Source-derived applied sequence

1. Big Why and Strategy Goal.
2. Strategy Segment.
3. Business Model.
4. Target Audience Insight.
5. Category Positioning.
6. Strategy Big Idea.
7. Functional Differentiators.
8. Emotional Differentiators.
9. Organizational Core Values.

## Evidence appendix required

- source and date for market/customer claims;
- assumptions and economic model;
- competing-factor scoring method;
- current vs future capabilities;
- contradictions and unresolved questions;
- validation plan.
