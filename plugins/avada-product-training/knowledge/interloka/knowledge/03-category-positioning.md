# Section 2 — Category Positioning

**Source range:** `IMG_7163`–`IMG_7175`.

## [SOURCE] Business model and category positioning

`IMG_7164` links operating pattern/know-how to category identity:

| Business pattern | Operating know-how | Category position example |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb/homestay | Design for trust | Home-sharing hotel |
| IKEA/DIY | Flat-pack | Affordable design furniture |
| Dell/build-to-order | Just-in-time | Custom-built computer |

Category positioning answers: **“The business/brand positions itself as what?”**

## [SOURCE] Category Entry Points and buying context

`IMG_7166` uses the CEP framework adapted from Sharp & Romaniuk:

- **Why** are they buying?
- **When** are they buying?
- **Where** are they buying?
- **With whom** are they buying?
- **With what** are they buying/consuming?

CEPs are memory cues buyers use when facing a buying situation. Only brands remembered in those situations enter consideration.

## [SOURCE] Product brand vs corporate brand

`IMG_7170` distinguishes:

- product/label: name the **product category**;
- corporate brand: name the **strategic industry/business category**.

Category positioning expresses a strategic vision, not merely a descriptive industry label.

## [SOURCE] Category-positioning format

The course provides a working format:

```text
Business-model/common category name (1)
+ a defining modifier/ABC (2)
```

Preferred global examples:

- Salesforce — cloud/subscription CRM;
- Airbnb — home-sharing marketplace;
- IKEA — affordable design furniture;
- Dell — custom-built computer;
- Olay — anti-aging skincare;
- Yellow Tail — accessible, fruity social wine.

## Global case library

- **Olay:** broadened the frame from a narrow treatment problem toward anti-aging skincare.
- **Salesforce:** cloud/subscription delivery was embedded in the category position, not only the pricing page.
- **Airbnb:** “home sharing” connected a marketplace business model to a buyer-understandable category.
- **IKEA:** category meaning is reinforced by the entire DIY/flat-pack activity system.

## [SYNTHESIS] Quality tests

- Does the category match how buyers search/compare?
- Does it express business-model reality?
- Is the modifier relevant in a buying situation?
- Does the category open a strategic space without becoming vague?
- Can product, operations, distribution, and communications consistently deliver it?
