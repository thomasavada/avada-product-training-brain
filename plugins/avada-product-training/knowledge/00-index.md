# Avada Product Training Brain — index

## Purpose

Brain này lưu methodology, curriculum, learner evidence và learning-loop của Avada Product Training. Nó giúp agent trả lời nhất quán về:

- business/ecommerce economics;
- merchant journey và stage;
- brand/app research;
- product mechanism, KPI, value, ROI và pricing;
- interactive training/case clinics;
- progress, learner feedback và curriculum correction.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer:

```text
primary/live source
→ exact artifact/transcript
→ archived learner artifact
→ dated synthesis
→ inference
```

Never reverse this hierarchy merely because a synthesis is easier to read.

## Known boundaries

- Full editable/rendered decks and learner binaries are not bundled; use the source repository in `sources/source-map.md`.
- Transcript ASR is noisy; timestamped direction is usable, exact names/numbers require re-verification.
- Feedback sample is two anonymous responses before Session 3.
- Learner progress is assessed from artifacts and observed behavior, not praise or attendance.
