## Evidence boundary

This is a learner analysis artifact, not an audited source. Its screenshots show what the team observed, but numbers and causal interpretations must be verified independently before reuse as instructor claims.

Claims requiring primary-source confirmation include, at minimum:

- approximately `$200M` 2024 revenue;
- `$350–400M` 2025 projection;
- `12B` TikTok views;
- 45-product portfolio count;
- store/distribution counts and dates;
- unit economics and the `$4–8` remaining per order;
- whether subscription/repeat is actually weak;
- any assertion that Bloom is profitable or unprofitable.

Use the deck to inspect learner reasoning and storytelling. Do not promote learner estimates into measured facts.
