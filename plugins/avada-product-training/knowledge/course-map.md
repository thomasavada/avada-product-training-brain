# Course map — Avada Product Training

## Thesis

AI makes implementation cheaper. Product teams must improve problem selection, merchant understanding, economics, viability proof and outcome ownership before accelerating delivery.

```text
Business foundation
→ merchant journey
→ stage × spend × leak
→ viable product argument
→ agent/human proof loops
→ production outcome
```

## Chapter 1 — understand ecommerce and the merchant

### Session 1 — Ecommerce fundamentals

Learners build commercial foundations before features:

1. Price anatomy, COGS and contribution.
2. Capacity, fixed cost, break-even and payback.
3. Traditional distribution versus ecommerce.
4. Customer ownership and channel conflict.
5. DTC versus B2C.
6. Offer, replenishment and price architecture.
7. Illustrative dropshipping economics.
8. Modernizing a niche through Bite/Graza cases.
9. Coffee P&L exercise.

Outcome: break down price, costs, contribution, capacity and distribution before product features.

### Session 2 — Merchant stage and stage-specific KPIs

Three operational stages:

- **Survival / Validate:** prove strangers buy and economics survive.
- **Repeatable Growth:** make acquisition, conversion, AOV and operations repeatable.
- **Scale / Compounding:** recover marginal leakage, increase CLV, expand distribution and improve reliability.

Teaching flow:

1. Stage transitions and operating behavior.
2. Store audits and predict-before-reveal.
3. AOV, CVR/CAC, ROAS/LTV and app-to-job maps.
4. Creative, creator/affiliate, repeat, loyalty, subscription, mobile, retail/Amazon and omnichannel.
5. Stage × solution fit.
6. Metric context, grain, window, lever and trade-off.

Outcome: identify operating stage and the KPI constraining growth before selecting a solution.

## Application clinic after Session 2

The content density created a capability gap: learners could recognize concepts but had not proved they could apply them.

Clinic contract:

```text
one real brand
→ independent observation
→ evidence + assumptions
→ group diagnosis
→ peer challenge
→ live correction
```

Cases:

- Blume skincare;
- Promix Nutrition;
- Bloom Nutrition.

Do not mix Blume and Bloom.

## Chapter 2 — build and ship the solution

### Session 3 — Build a solution and calculate ROI

Actual 27 October session:

1. Team Bloom learner presentation and critique.
2. Stage and ICP before solution.
3. Alia/value model: same mechanism, different merchant volume and value ceiling.
4. Expensive/cheap relative to value delivered.
5. Surface metric versus business outcome.
6. Assignment: choose one app and explain problem, metric, mechanism, value, pricing and proof.

Redesigned deck flow:

1. Merchant journey, spend pools and revealed demand.
2. Case-clinic protocol: inspect → predict → argue → reveal → decide.
3. Alia/Graza deep dive.
4. Order Editing external follow-on deep dive.
5. Opportunity contract and product thesis.
6. ROI models and pricing.

Outcome: turn a stage-specific KPI leak into a mechanism, model contribution, state evidence limits and defend a proof plan.

### Session 4 — AI-native operating model and shipping

1. Start from a measurable business goal.
2. Replace handoff factory with proof loops.
3. Share ownership across BA/PM/PO, Engineering and QC.
4. Prototype and prove viability before production polish.
5. Treat Agent Experience/MCP as a product surface.
6. Headless acceptance: inspect → configure → execute → observe → read back → fail safely.
7. Ship, observe and iterate against the goal.

Outcome: derive acceptance criteria from the goal, use AI without outsourcing judgment and verify the shipped outcome.

## Curriculum corrections after feedback

Two anonymous responses from 21/24 October preceded Session 3:

- use Kahoot as a foundational diagnostic;
- reduce Session 2 density;
- show one worked case end to end;
- provide scaffold/worksheet;
- bridge theory to real-brand application.

Session 3 already addressed part of this through the Bloom case and reduced new content. Next iteration should use spaced practice rather than trying to complete business sense in four to six sessions.
