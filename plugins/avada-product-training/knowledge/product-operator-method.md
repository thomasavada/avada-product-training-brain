# Product operator method

## Canonical chain

```text
Research real business
→ understand merchant stage and spend
→ identify a literal KPI/leak
→ model eligible volume and economics
→ explain mechanism and journey
→ define evidence boundary
→ prototype/pilot
→ ship, instrument and own outcome
```

## 1. Start with business truth

Before feature ideation, establish:

- who the merchant/customer is;
- operating stage;
- customer journey moment;
- current workflow and workaround;
- baseline metric, grain and time window;
- spend pool and economic leak;
- why the current system is insufficient.

## 2. Diagnose stage by behavior

Revenue band is context, not definition.

| Stage | Primary job | Typical proof |
|---|---|---|
| Survival | prove demand and viable unit economics | strangers buy, product/offer works, cash survives |
| Growth | make growth repeatable | CAC/CVR/AOV/creative/operations become systems |
| Scale | compound and recover marginal leakage | CLV, reliability, channel coverage, orchestration |

Do not sell a category because Avada owns an app there. Sell the current bottleneck.

## 3. Read each metric at calculation depth

For any metric require:

1. Definition.
2. Formula.
3. Source and grain.
4. Denominator.
5. Baseline/window.
6. Meaning when high/low.
7. Lever/mechanism.
8. Trade-offs/guardrails.
9. Economic value per movement.

## 4. Map direct spend and full economic pool

Direct software spend is only visible budget. Also map:

- ads/creator spend;
- discounts;
- payment/logistics/returns;
- support headcount;
- downtime and risk;
- lost contribution.

Competitor/app density is revealed-demand evidence, not market-size proof.

## 5. Opportunity contract

Minimum contract:

```text
stage + ICP
× journey/spend surface
× economic leak
→ mechanism
→ KPI movement
→ modeled contribution
→ evidence quality
→ pricing ceiling
→ proof / stop rule
```

No baseline + mechanism + value means an idea, not a product thesis.

## 6. Value and pricing

Price is relative to merchant value, not sticker price.

```text
eligible volume
× expected lift
× contribution per outcome
× confidence
= risk-adjusted modeled value
```

Pricing ceiling also depends on alternatives, friction, risk, cost-to-serve and value-capture share.

Never call modeled/associated value incremental without a suitable control/holdout.

## 7. Viability before completion

Build only after demonstrating:

- real problem/ICP evidence;
- stage and spend fit;
- KPI/ROI math;
- mechanism;
- prototype or runnable demo;
- failure criteria;
- measurement plan.

A bug-free implementation can still be a failed product.

## 8. AI role

AI may:

- search and compare sources;
- calculate;
- challenge assumptions;
- validate internal consistency;
- draft alternate hypotheses.

AI must not replace the observation or judgment the learner/product owner is meant to build.

## Output contract

For any product/app analysis return:

- Problem/ICP/stage.
- Observed evidence.
- Hypothesis/mechanism.
- Metric equation and economic model.
- Assumptions and unknowns.
- Risks/guardrails.
- Proof plan and stop rule.
- Verdict: Build / Test more / Reposition / Stop.
