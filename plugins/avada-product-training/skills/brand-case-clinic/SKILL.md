# Brand Case Clinic

## Read first

- `knowledge/product-operator-method.md`
- `knowledge/interactive-training.md`
- relevant Chase/Interloka notes based on the case
- `knowledge/principles/evidence-before-confidence.md`

## Flow

### 1. Inspect before diagnosis

Collect live evidence:

- official site/PDP/cart/checkout;
- offer and pricing;
- retail/marketplace presence;
- ads/creator/social evidence;
- reviews/UGC;
- lifecycle/subscription/loyalty;
- credible company/revenue sources when available.

Do not show a complete diagnosis before the learner commits observations.

### 2. Build evidence ledger

For every claim record:

```text
claim
source URL/artifact
observed date
status: fact / inference / assumption / unknown
```

### 3. Diagnose

Answer:

1. What is the customer truly buying?
2. How is it sold: product, offer, channel and proof?
3. What is the distribution system?
4. What stage is the business in and what evidence supports it?
5. What economics can be estimated safely?
6. What is the next constraint?
7. What hypothesis deserves a test?

### 4. Use contrast

Compare at least one competitor/substitute on:

- business model;
- offer;
- channel/distribution;
- price/economics;
- category/positioning;
- constraint.

Do not compare feature lists alone.

### 5. Challenge

Ask peers to identify:

- strongest observed fact;
- weakest assumption;
- contradictory evidence;
- missing denominator/grain;
- alternate stage diagnosis;
- test that would change the verdict.

### 6. Revise

Return a before/after conclusion showing what changed after challenge.

## Output

- One-sentence thesis.
- Evidence table.
- Stage/constraint diagnosis.
- Distribution and offer map.
- Economics range and sensitivity.
- Competitor contrast.
- Hypothesis + cheapest discriminating test.
- Fact/inference/unknown appendix.

## Boundaries

Learner slides are evidence of learner reasoning, not audited truth. Revenue estimates, channel counts and causal claims require primary-source verification.
